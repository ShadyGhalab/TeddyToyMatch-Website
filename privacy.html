Privacy Policy

Teddy Toy Match (Teddy Dreams: Match 3 Puzzle on the stores) is a puzzle game by Tendonlife for iPhone and iPad (App Store) and Android (Google Play). This policy explains what information the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have.

No Account Needed

You can play without creating an account or giving us your name, email address or phone number. Your progress, settings, room decorations and owned purchases are saved on your device. We don’t run our own servers for the game, so we don’t keep a copy of your save.

Information Collected Automatically

The game uses the services below. When you play, they may collect:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language and similar technical details.
  • Identifiers: identifiers for this app installation, and your device’s advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or on iPhone the IDFA if you allow tracking).
  • Approximate location: country, region or city worked out from your IP address. The game never asks for or uses your precise location.
  • Gameplay and app activity: for example levels started and finished, screens opened, settings changed, purchases started and completed, and ads shown or watched.
  • Crash reports and diagnostics: what the app was doing when it crashed, plus performance information such as launch time.

Ads and Your Consent

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: banners on the menus and optional rewarded ads that give extra moves. AdMob uses the information above to show, measure and protect ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google’s consent form asks for your choices before personalized ads are shown. You can change them at any time in Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • On iPhone, ads are personalized only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this in your iPhone’s Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device’s settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Buying Remove Ads hides banner ads.

Analytics, Crash Reports and Attribution

  • Google Analytics for Firebase helps us understand how the game is played, for example which levels are hard, so we can improve it.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports so we can fix bugs.
  • AppsFlyer measures which ads or campaigns led to installs, measures ad performance, and helps prevent fraud.

Game Center, Google Play Games and Purchases

  • Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center on iPhone and Google Play Games on Android, if you use them. Apple and Google handle your player profile, scores and achievements under their own privacy policies.
  • Purchases and restoring purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card details. We receive confirmation of what you bought so we can unlock it.

How We Use Information

To run the game and save your progress; to show ads and give rewards; to unlock and restore purchases; to understand how the game is played and improve its balance; to find and fix crashes; to measure our marketing; to prevent fraud and abuse; and to answer your messages.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers named above, which process it to provide their services to us, and when the law requires it. Google may also use AdMob data for its own advertising purposes as described in its policies, subject to your consent choices. We don’t sell your personal information.

How Long Information Is Kept

Information on your device stays until you reset your progress or delete the game. Information collected by our service providers is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above, according to each provider’s retention settings.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Change ad consent in Settings → Privacy Choices (where offered), the iPhone tracking setting, or your Android Advertising ID settings.
  • Reset your progress in Settings, or delete the game to remove everything stored on your device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information or object to its use. Email us and we’ll help.

Children

Teddy Toy Match is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and information was collected, please contact us.

Security and International Transfers

Our service providers encrypt the information in transit. They may process it in countries other than yours, under safeguards required by law.

Changes

We’ll post any changes to this policy here and update the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Support@teddytoymatch.com.
