Need a hand?

Google Play says the game is unavailable.

Make sure you joined the group and opted in to Teddy Dreams using the same Google account. Availability depends on the active release, your country and device compatibility. Contact support if you still cannot access the test.

Do I have to send my email address?

No. Google Groups and Google Play manage enrollment. This website does not collect your email address or require an additional account.

I'm already in the Android testing community.

You do not need to join the group again. Open the Teddy Dreams opt-in link, become a tester, and then install the game.

How can I report a bug?

Email Feedback@teddytoymatch.com with your device model, Android version, and the problem you encountered. Do not include private account or payment information.
